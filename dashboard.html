Key business insights (full dataset)

  1. Furniture is a profit trap. Sales of $742K (32% of total) yield only $18K profit (2.5% margin) vs ~17% for Technology and Office Supplies.
  2. Discounts above 20% destroy profit. Lines discounted over 20% lost $135K combined (1,393 lines); undiscounted lines earned $321K. Heavily discounted Tables alone lose $17.7K despite $207K sales.
  3. Central region underperforms. Its margin is 7.9% vs 14.9% in West, and it gives the deepest average discount (24% vs 11% in West). Central lost $52K on lines discounted over 20%.
  4. Sales are strongly seasonal. Sep, Nov, Dec and Mar hold 52% of annual sales; November is the peak ($352K). Sales grew 29.5% in 2017 and 20.4% in 2018.
  5. Technology copiers are the profit engine. Copiers earn $55.6K at 37% margin on only 234 units; Canon imageCLASS 2200 alone makes $25.2K. Meanwhile 301 of 1,850 products lose money, totalling –$77K.
Recommendations: cap discounts at ~20% (esp. Furniture/Tables/Bookcases, Central); reprice or drop loss-making products; build inventory and campaigns ahead of Sep–Dec; push Copiers/Accessories/Paper where margins are 25–43%.
